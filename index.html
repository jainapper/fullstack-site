1. Our commitment to privacy

At Fullstack Fulfillment, we understand that trust is fundamental to successful logistics partnerships. This Privacy Policy explains how Fullstack Fulfilment Australia Pty Ltd collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you use our services or visit fullstackfs.com.au. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Business information

When you engage our services — including through our quote and onboarding forms — we collect company details (ABN/ACN, business name, trading names), contact information (names, email addresses, phone numbers), billing and shipping addresses, financial information for payment processing, and product information including SKU and inventory details.

2.2 Operational data

Through our systems and service delivery, we collect order and shipment data, inventory levels and movements, performance metrics and service analytics, and communication records and support tickets.

2.3 Website information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect IP addresses and browser information, pages visited and interaction data, and cookies and similar technologies (with your consent where required).

3. How we use your information

  • Providing services: processing orders, managing inventory, coordinating shipments, and delivering our 4PL solutions

  • Communications: sending service updates, responding to enquiries, and providing customer support
  • Compliance: meeting legal obligations, maintaining records, and ensuring regulatory compliance
  • Business development: analysing service performance to better serve your needs

4. Information sharing and disclosure

4.1 Service partners

We share necessary information with shipping carriers and freight partners to facilitate deliveries (including DHL, Australia Post, NZ Post, SEKO Logistics, and Parcel Right), technology partners for system integrations (including Extensiv and Starshipit), and professional service providers (legal, accounting, insurance).

4.2 Overseas disclosure

Where you use international delivery services, shipment information is necessarily disclosed to overseas carriers and their agents in the destination country (for example, New Zealand for NZ Post deliveries). Some technology partners may store data on servers located outside Australia.

4.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred to the successor entity.

4.4 No sale of information

We never sell your personal or business information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data security

We implement security measures including encrypted data transmission and storage, secure facility access controls, regular security reviews, staff training on data protection, and incident response procedures. While we maintain robust security practices, no system is completely impenetrable; we continuously work to protect your information against unauthorised access.

You have the right to access information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, opt out of marketing communications, request deletion of information (subject to legal retention requirements), and lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

7. Data retention
